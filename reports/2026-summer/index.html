Method and reuse

One source gets one call per transfer for an interest, talks, agreed or medical claim dated before resolution. Completed = hit; collapsed = miss; pending = excluded from the rate. Score = round(100 × (hits + 2) / (resolved + 4)). Multiple sources may call the same deal. The call CSV shows the earliest qualifying evidence for each source/deal pair.
